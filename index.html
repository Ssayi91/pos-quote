Choosing Your Workflow

The software is identical. The hardware dictates your floor dynamics.

We offer two hardware configurations. Both run the exact same powerful software, connect to the same central database, and print to the same thermal printer. The choice depends entirely on how your team prefers to operate.

Our goal is to educate you on the operational differences so you can select the setup that best matches your restaurant's unique flow, staff size, and service style.

The Mobility Factor

A tablet is not tethered to a wall. It allows waiters to take orders directly at the table, eliminating the need to walk back and forth to a fixed terminal. This is highly dependent on your number of active waiters and table layout.

The Station Factor

A fixed touchscreen provides a dedicated, stable command center. Some teams prefer this for speed, as it offers a larger interface and prevents devices from being misplaced during a busy rush.

The Vision

One unified system. Total operational clarity.

Regardless of the hardware you choose, the outcome is the same: a streamlined operation where data flows seamlessly from the table to the kitchen, and finally to your management reports.
